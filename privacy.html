Privacy Policy

VNGLS · last updated 20 September 2026

This policy covers vng-artist, a private command-line tool used by the VNGLS artist account to render and publish its own music videos.

What is collected

Nothing is collected from anyone. The tool has no users other than VNGLS itself, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no server that receives data.

What is stored, and where

When VNGLS signs in to TikTok, the resulting access and refresh tokens are written to a file on the artist's own computer, readable only by that user. They are never transmitted anywhere except to TikTok's own API, and deleting the file removes them.

What is sent to TikTok

Only what is needed to publish: the video file itself, its caption, and the chosen privacy setting. The tool reads the creator's username and permitted privacy levels so it can show them before uploading.

Third parties

No data is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party. There are no advertising, analytics or profiling services in the tool.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from TikTok's own settings, under Security and permissions, or by deleting the local token file.
