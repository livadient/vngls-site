About

For years VNGLS wrote in an old notebook and showed it to nobody — the one at the back of the class, in the kind of silence that keeps a name hidden.

That name turns up in both of the first songs, from opposite ends. Γίναμε Τρεις was written at night in a child's room, listening to a name in the quiet — a song about a night turning into a morning, and about love that doesn't get divided when a family grows. Τώρα βρήκα φως is the same story told backwards: the counting of minutes until a day was over, and the stubbornness that outlasted them. One of those changes had to be earned. The other arrived without asking.

Both songs are unreleased, and an album named after the second is on the way. Each is finished with the words on screen in both languages, so they can travel as far as the music.
